Prospecting
Identify and approach potential clients through outbound channels, referrals, and industry presence. Goal is a qualified first conversation.
Stage 1 Top of Funnel
Outreach Channels
LinkedIn — direct message to hardware founders, ops leads, engineering directors
Referrals from existing clients and factory partners
Industry events, trade shows, accelerators
Inbound leads via website / content
Cold email to target company ICP list
Qualification Signals
Has an active hardware product in development
Working with or looking for CM / ODM / factories in Asia
Pain around factory communication, quality, or documentation
Budget authority confirmed or within reach
Timeline is active — not exploratory only
Stage Exit Criteria
First meeting booked
Basic ICP fit confirmed
Contact logged in CRM / contact log
Do not proceed if no active project or no budget signal
Lead Generation & Discovery
Run a structured discovery process to understand the client's product, supply chain situation, pain points, and what SCM Bridge can solve for them.
Stage 2 Discovery
Discovery Call Agenda
Introduce SCM Bridge OS — services and process overview
Understand current product stage (Idea / PoC / Design / Test / MP)
Map existing factory relationships and pain points
Understand team structure — internal vs. outsourced engineering
Identify target markets and regulatory requirements
Understand timeline and volume targets
Key Questions to Answer
What does the client need help with — factory finding, QC, docs, translation, all?
Do they have a CM / ODM already? How is that relationship?
What assets do they have ready — CAD, BOM, drawings, firmware?
What have they tried before that failed?
Who signs off on scope and budget?
Stage Exit Criteria
Discovery notes written up and filed
Fit assessment completed — proceed / no-go
Decision-maker identified
Initial scope and service areas agreed verbally
Flag if client has unrealistic timeline or budget mismatch
Client Onboarding
Formal kickoff with the new client. Set up the project infrastructure, introduce the team, collect all background materials, and align on working norms.
Stage 3 Kickoff
Kickoff Meeting Agenda
Introductions — SCM Bridge team and client team
Review agreed scope and deliverables
Walk through the Phase Map — confirm which phase the project is in
Review communication norms — cadence, channels, response times
Confirm escalation path and key contacts
Set up shared folder structure and tools access
Setup Checklist
Google Drive project folder created and shared
Contact log opened with client-side names and roles
Project Asset Registry initialized
Client NDA executed and filed
Existing assets collected — BOM, drawings, vendor list
Project brief written and filed
Stage Exit Criteria
Kickoff meeting complete and notes sent
Drive folder live and populated
Client NDA signed
Existing assets received and logged in Registry
SOW drafting initiated
Statement of Work
Define the full engagement scope, deliverables, timeline, pricing, and sign-off process. The SOW is the contractual anchor for everything that follows.
Stage 4 Contract
SOW Sections
Project background and context
Objectives and success criteria
Scope of work — services and deliverables listed explicitly
Out-of-scope items (to manage expectations)
Timeline and key milestones
Pricing, payment terms, and invoicing schedule
Change order process
Client responsibilities and dependencies
Pricing Approach
Fixed fee for defined deliverable sets
Retainer for ongoing support (monthly)
Time & materials for open-ended advisory
Travel and expenses — pass-through at cost
NRE costs from vendors — quoted separately
Stage Exit Criteria
SOW reviewed internally before sending
Client receives SOW with 5-day review window
Revisions addressed and reissued if needed
SOW signed by both parties
First invoice issued or deposit received
Project activated — execution begins
Typical SOW Components at a Glance
Factory identification & shortlisting
NDA execution with vendor(s)
Asset package preparation & handoff
RFQ management — quotes, comparison, negotiation
DFM review facilitation
QC and inspection oversight
Translation and communication liaison
EVT / DVT / PVT support
Logistics and shipment coordination
Weekly status reports

Validate the opportunity, align stakeholders, select partners, and establish the project foundation before any engineering begins.
Gate: DR0 — Concept Review
PM Actions — in order
1
Define the problem and confirm the market need
2
Draft the Market Requirements Document (MRD)
Include market context, user stories, and success criteria
3
Schedule and run the DR0 stakeholder review
4
Collect stakeholder sign-off on the MRD
5
Identify and shortlist manufacturing and service partners
6
Circulate and execute NDAs with all selected partners
7
Create the Google Drive project folder structure
8
Open the project contact log

Proof of Concept Phase
Reduce technical and market risk by validating core technology and locking architecture before committing to full design.
Gate: DR1 — Preliminary Design Review
PM Actions — in order
1
Assemble the PoC engineering team and brief on requirements
2
Kick off market validation activities
3
Source components and engineering services for the PoC
4
Explore off-the-shelf alternatives before custom design
5
Build the non-form-factor PoC sample
6
Test PoC against core technical requirements — document results
7
Draft the PRD based on PoC and market validation findings
8
Prepare the preliminary BOM — flag all long-lead items
9
Confirm factory partners for the full design phase
10
Schedule and run the DR1 review meeting
Deliverables
PoC sample — built and tested
Market validation report
PRD — drafted and approved internally
Preliminary BOM with long-lead flags
Factory partner confirmation
System interface diagram
Gate & Resources

✓ PoC sample built and tested
✓ Market validation done
✓ PRD approved internally
✓ Preliminary BOM drafted
✓ Factory partners confirmed
DR1 passed? Move to
Design Phase
Coordinate all engineering disciplines to complete design, gather DFM feedback, and produce manufacturer-ready file packages.
Gate: DR2 — Critical Design Review
PM Actions — in order
1
Formally brief ME, EE, SW, and Packaging design teams
2
Coordinate DFM review sessions with CM/ODM during design — not after
Early DFM feedback prevents costly late-stage redesigns
3
Track ME assets checklist to completion with the ME team
4
Track EE assets checklist to completion with the EE team
5
Compile the Technical Requirements Document (TRD)
6
Confirm all DFM feedback is incorporated into the design
7
Lock the master BOM with all MPNs and alternates confirmed
8
Build handoff manifests for CM and ODM using the Manifest Builder
9
Complete IP review with legal
10
Schedule and run the DR2 review meeting
Deliverables
ME asset checklist — signed off
EE asset checklist — signed off
TRD — compiled and reviewed
DFM feedback incorporated (documented)
Master BOM with MPNs locked
Handoff manifests — CM and ODM
IP review sign-off
Gate & Resources
DR2 Gate Criteria
✓ ME + EE checklists signed off
✓ All drawings released
✓ TRD reviewed and approved
✓ DFM feedback incorporated
✓ IP review complete

DR2 passed? Move to
Test Phase
Send files to factory, build and validate first physical prototypes, complete regulatory certifications, and freeze the BOM.
Gate: DR3 · EVT · DVT
PM Actions — in order
1
Send complete handoff packages to CM and ODM with signed manifests
2
Coordinate and attend the EVT build at factory
3
Document all EVT results — track every pass/fail item to resolution
4
Issue updated design package for DVT once EVT issues are resolved
5
Oversee DVT — reliability testing and regulatory compliance verification
6
Commission and supervise the First Article Inspection (FAI)
7
Submit certifications for all target markets (CE, FCC, RoHS, etc.)
8
Complete vendor audit
9
Freeze the BOM on DVT pass — no further changes without ECO
10
Schedule and run the DR3 review meeting
Deliverables
CM + ODM handoff packages sent (with manifests)
EVT report — pass/fail documented
DVT report — reliability and regulatory
FAI report — signed off
Vendor audit report
Certifications obtained for all markets
BOM frozen

✓ DVT passed

Factory Phase
Set up and validate the production line, run PVT to confirm yield and quality, then confirm the factory is ready for mass production.
Gate: DR4 · PVT
PM Actions — in order
1
Review and sign off production SOPs with CM
2
Validate all test fixtures on the production line
3
Verify operator training records are complete
4
Run the PVT (Production Validation Test) build
5
Review PVT results against yield and quality targets
Resolve all failures before signing off
6
Activate QC inline inspection process
7
Confirm capacity plan meets the MP production schedule
8
Obtain official MP readiness sign-off from factory
9
Schedule and run the DR4 review meeting
Deliverables
Production SOPs — reviewed and approved
Test fixtures — validated on line
Operator training records — verified
PVT report — yield targets met
QC inline inspection plan — active
Capacity confirmation
MP readiness sign-off

3
Set up yield and quality metrics tracking

Activate final inspection process for outgoing goods

Arrange logistics, freight, and shipping for first delivery
6
Establish the 8D failure analysis process for quality issues
7
Keep the project contact log current throughout MP
8
Track and communicate all ECOs to factory — document every change
9
Log all quality escapes with 8D reference numbers
10
Conduct periodic production line reviews to optimize yield

Quick Reference
Key Rules
NDA before any IP leaves the building
Every delivery gets a Handoff Manifest
Log every vendor contact in the contact log
SOW signed before internal work begins
No verbal agreements — everything in writing
BOM frozen after DVT — ECO required for changes
