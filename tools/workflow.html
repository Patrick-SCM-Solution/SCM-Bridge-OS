Cold Outreach
Apollo 7-Step Framework — Show Me You Know Me (#SMYKM). Research first, always. Goal is a reply, not a close.
No calendar links in first email
The 7 Steps
1
Subject Line — SMYKM. Personal detail + implied problem.
"Paul's aluminum sourcing + Stoney Creek fire rescue" — not "Quick question"
2
First Sentence — non-salesy opener. Prove you looked them up.
"I noticed you closed your Series A last month." — not "I hope this finds you well."
3
Transition — "The real reason for my outreach, however, is —"
Bridges the hook to the business problem without feeling like bait-and-switch.
4
Challenge — suspect a pain point. Hypothesis, not certainty.
"Given your fire rescue vertical, I suspect sourcing stable aluminum vendors is harder than it should be."
5
Value Prop — ONE angle: hook, pain insight, or loss aversion.
Loss aversion is 2× more powerful than gain framing. "Every month without a second source is a production risk."
6
Objection Handle — pre-handle the obvious objection.
"I know you likely have vendor relationships. What we do differently is continuous qualification."
7
Close — end with a question, never a calendar link.
"Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes?" — sign off: "Cheers!" or "Thanks!"
Follow-Up Cadence
D0
Send initial SMYKM email
D3
First follow-up — reply to same thread, one sentence
"Wanted to bump this in case it got buried — thoughts?"
D7
Second follow-up — add a new value hook
"We recently helped a similar company qualify a new CM in Vietnam in under 8 weeks."
D14
Break-up email — graceful close, no pressure
"I'll stop here so I'm not adding noise. If [pain] ever becomes urgent, happy to reconnect."
+90d
Tag in Odoo for 90-day re-check
Situations change. A graceful walk-away often gets a reply within 30 days.
Walk Away From Outreach If
No response after 4 touches
"Not the right person" with no referral offered
"Happy with current setup" — no pain signal
Decision-maker won't engage at all
BANT Discovery Call
Junior BD leads. Senior BD (Patrick / Kara) observes silently on mute. 30–45 min. Goal is diagnosis — not pitch. Log Odoo BANT record within 2 hours.
Don't pitch. Listen for pain.
Opening Line
"Thanks for making the time. Before I say anything about what we do, I want to understand your situation. What's the main supply chain challenge you're dealing with right now?"
Phase Mapping — always anchor to the Phase Map
→ Ask: "Which phase are you in right now — Idea, PoC, Design, Test, Factory, or MP?"
→ Use the Phase Map as a shared reference — it anchors the conversation and signals your process fluency
→ The phase determines scope, timeline, and which services apply — log it in the BANT record

B
Budget
What budget have you allocated for solving this?
▼
Follow-Up Probes
"Is that budget already approved, or does it need sign-off?"
"Is this an ops budget, capex line, or project budget?"
"What's the approval process — one sign-off or multiple layers?"
"What would you expect to pay to have this fully resolved?"
Green Light
✓ Specific number, approved or clear path to approval
Red Flag
"We don't have budget right now"
"We were hoping this would be cheap"
A
Authority
Who else needs to be involved in a decision like this?
▼
Follow-Up Probes
"Is the final call yours to make, or does it go up the chain?"
"Does procurement or finance need to sign off?"
"Who would veto this if they disagreed?"
"Are there other stakeholders we should loop in early?"
Green Light
✓ DM on call, or strong influencer with a clear path to DM
Red Flag
"My boss decides" — no path to that person
Influencer only, DM refuses to engage
N
Need
What happens if you don't solve this problem?
▼
Follow-Up Probes
"What does this cost you right now — dollars, time, or risk?"
"Has this caused operational failures — missed deliveries, quality escapes?"
"Is this a 'nice to fix' or a 'must fix before [event]'?"
"How long has this been a problem? What changed that makes now the moment?"
Green Light
✓ Financial or operational urgency articulated, specific event driving action
Red Flag
"It would be nice to improve this someday"
No operational or financial consequence if unsolved
T
Timeline
When do you need this solved by?
▼
Follow-Up Probes
"Is there an external deadline driving that — contract, launch, audit?"
"What happens if you miss that date?"
"How long have you been looking at this? Why now?"
"If we could start next month, would you be ready to move?"
Green Light
✓ Specific date or hard external trigger (contract, launch, production start)
Red Flag
"Eventually" / "next year" / "when the time is right"
No external trigger — purely internal and soft

Senior BD (Patrick or Kara) reviews the BANT log and makes the call. Junior BD never decides independently. Decision within 24 hrs of log submission.

? Maybe 1 MORE Q

Decline Templates
General Walk-Away
"After our conversation, I don't think we're the right fit for your timeline and scope right now — our process is thorough, and rushing it doesn't serve you well. Happy to reconnect in [timeframe]. Good luck with [specific thing they mentioned]."
No Timeline
"Without a clear timeline driving this, we wouldn't be adding value right now. When [event or urgency] shows up, reach back out — we'd love to help."

Target Verticals
Hardware Startups
Post-prototype, pre-scale. Founder is DM. Taiwan / Vietnam manufacturing. Budget = seed/Series A capex. Timeline = investor milestones.
Consumer Electronics
CM quality or pricing pain, DFM friction. Need: second-source or new CM. Timeline = product launch or retailer window.
Medical Devices
ISO 13485 / FDA-aware sourcing. Supplier documentation gaps. Timeline = submission date (hard wall). Authority = VP Supply Chain + Regulatory.
EV / Auto Components
Tier 2/3 supplier scaling. SOP date = non-negotiable. Need: second-source before ramp. Authority = Procurement + Engineering joint decision.
Industrial / B2B
Safety-critical, reliability-driven. Vendor instability or single-source risk. Value-based buyers. (ref: Motis Fire Rescue)
