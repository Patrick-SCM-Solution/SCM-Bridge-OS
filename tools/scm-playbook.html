Lead Generation & Discovery
Run a structured discovery process to understand the client's product, supply chain situation, pain points, and what SCM Bridge can solve for them.
Stage 2 Discovery
Discovery Call Agenda
Introduce SCM Bridge OS — services and process overview
Understand current product stage (Idea / PoC / Design / Test / MP)

Understand team structure — internal vs. outsourced engineering

Key Questions to Answer
What does the client need help with — factory finding, QC, docs, translation, all?

What assets do they have ready — CAD, BOM, drawings, firmware?
What have they tried before that failed?

Vendor Outreach
Identify and contact factories, CMs, ODMs, or specialist suppliers that match the client's product requirements. Build a shortlist before any confidential information is shared.
Stage 1 Sourcing
Vendor Identification
Match product category to known factory network
Check volume requirements vs. factory MOQ and capacity
Assess quality certifications (ISO, IATF, etc.) needed
Review factory location for logistics and lead time
Screen for blacklisted or previously problematic vendors
First Contact Protocol
Send introduction — SCM Bridge role and client context (no client name yet)
Provide product category and volume range only
Confirm capability and interest before sharing further
Log response date and contact person in contact log
Send NDA as next step if capability confirmed
Stage Exit Criteria
Minimum 2–3 qualified vendors identified
Capability and interest confirmed in writing
All vendor contacts logged
Vendor shortlist reviewed with client
NDA Handoff
Execute mutual NDA with each selected vendor before sharing any client confidential information — drawings, BOM, firmware, or product specifications.
Stage 2 IP Protected
NDA Process
Send SCM Bridge / client NDA template to vendor
Allow 5 business days for review and redlines
Resolve any redlines — escalate to client if needed
Collect countersigned copy
File signed NDA in Drive — client folder and vendor folder
Log NDA execution date and expiry in contact log
What Requires NDA Coverage
All mechanical drawings and 3D CAD files
All EE schematics, Gerbers, firmware source
BOM with MPNs (identifies product architecture)
Product photos or renders (pre-launch)
Client company name and brand (in some cases)
Target markets, launch dates, volume plans
Stage Exit Criteria
NDA signed by all parties on vendor shortlist
Signed copies filed in Drive (both sides)
Expiry dates logged and calendar reminder set
Client informed NDA is in place — asset sharing approved
IP Reminder: Never share design files, BOM with MPNs, or product photos with any vendor before NDA is countersigned and filed. "Verbal NDAs" and "we trust them" are not acceptable. If a vendor refuses to sign, escalate to client before proceeding.
Asset Delivery
Package and deliver the correct asset set to each vendor using the Handoff Manifest Builder. Track what was sent, to whom, and when. Every delivery gets a manifest.
Stage 3 Delivery
Asset Package Contents
ME pack — STEP, drawings (PDF), DXF, tolerance specs
EE pack — schematics, Gerbers, BOM, CPL, test spec
CMF pack — color specs, material specs, finish samples
Tooling pack — mold design, T1 requirements, tool ownership docs
Packaging pack — dieline, artwork, regulatory marks
TRD — Technical Requirements Document
Delivery Protocol
Use Handoff Manifest Builder to generate manifest PDF
Share files via Drive shared link — never email attachments for large packs
Send manifest to vendor with cover message in both English and Chinese
Request written acknowledgment of receipt
Log delivery in Project Asset Registry — date, vendor, rev
Set follow-up ping for DFM response (target: 5–7 business days)
Stage Exit Criteria
Manifest generated for each vendor delivery
Files confirmed received by vendor
Delivery logged in Asset Registry
DFM / quote timeline agreed with vendor
Client notified — delivery complete
Active Engagement
Ongoing project management and liaison work — from first DFM feedback through RFQ, sample builds, testing, and into mass production as defined by the SOW.
Stage 4 Active
Ongoing Tasks
Collect and translate DFM feedback from vendors
Facilitate RFQ — manage quotes, comparison sheet, negotiation
Issue POs and track PIs with vendors
Coordinate sample builds — EVT / DVT / PVT
Oversee QC, inspection, and FAI
Manage ECO distribution when designs change
Coordinate logistics and shipping
Reporting Cadence
Weekly status update to client (written summary)
Bi-weekly or monthly review call (per SOW)
Issue log maintained — open items tracked
8D report initiated for any quality failures
All vendor communications BCC'd or forwarded to Drive
Project Close Criteria
All SOW deliverables completed and accepted
Final report issued to client
Asset Registry updated to final rev
Drive folder archived and access confirmed
Final invoice issued and settled
Lessons-learned note filed internally
